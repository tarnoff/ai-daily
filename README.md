# AI Daily

A two-host daily briefing on AI, including health.

## Podcast feed

https://tarnoff.github.io/ai-daily/feed.xml

## Add it in Apple Podcasts

On iPhone or iPad:

1. Open the Podcasts app and go to Library.
2. Tap the "..." (More) button at the top right, then tap "Follow a Show by URL..."
3. Paste the feed URL above and tap Follow.

On a Mac:

1. Open the Podcasts app.
2. In the menu bar, choose File, then "Follow a Show by URL..."
3. Paste the feed URL above and click Follow.

New episodes appear automatically once they are added to the feed.

## Files

- feed.xml: the RSS feed
- episodes/: episode MP3 files
- cover.jpg: show artwork
